About the College

Michael Fretheim College was built in 2005 in Michaeltown, Fretheimistan. It was named after Michael Fretheim, who wanted to build this school in order to complete his Week 3 Web Systems homework.

Location

Our main campus is located in Michaeltown, the capital of the greatest country in the world, Fretheimistan. The campus as several buildings, restaurants, a library and student housing for those who want to live on campus. There is also a 20 foot tall statue of our glorious founder Michael Fretheim.
